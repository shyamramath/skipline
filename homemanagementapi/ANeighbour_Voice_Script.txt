ANeighbour App - Voice Recording Script
========================================

[INTRO - Warm, welcoming tone]

Welcome to ANeighbour — your trusted partner in home management.

---

[SECTION 1: What is ANeighbour]

So, what exactly is ANeighbour?

ANeighbour is a comprehensive home management platform designed to help homeowners keep track of their properties in one convenient place. Whether you own a single home or manage multiple properties, ANeighbour gives you instant access to all your property details — from owner information and tax assessments to property features and location data.

Think of it as your digital home assistant — organized, accessible, and always up to date.

---

[SECTION 2: How to Onboard and Add Your Home]

Getting started with ANeighbour is simple.

First, visit our website and sign in using your Google account. This secure login keeps your information safe while making access quick and easy.

Once you're logged in, you're ready to add your first property.

Simply enter your property address, and ANeighbour will automatically pull detailed information about your home — including the owner name, property features.

Review the details, confirm everything looks correct, and click Save.

That's it! Your home is now part of your ANeighbour inventory. You'll even receive a confirmation email with all your property details.

---

[SECTION 3: How ANeighbour Works]

Now, let's talk about how ANeighbour works for you.

Once your home is added, you can access your property dashboard anytime. Here's what you can do:

View Property Details — See comprehensive information about your home, including square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, year built, and lot size.

Track Owner Information — Keep owner records and mailing addresses organized and accessible.

Access Tax Assessments — Review property tax information and assessed values all in one place.

Generate QR Codes — Each property gets a unique QR code that links directly to its details — perfect for sharing or quick reference.

Stay Notified — When you add a new property, you'll receive instant email and SMS notifications confirming the addition.

And if you ever have questions, our AI-powered chat assistant is available to help guide you through any home management questions you might have.

---

ANeighbour — making home management simple, organized, and stress-free.

Sign up today and take control of your property information.

Thank you for choosing ANeighbour.
